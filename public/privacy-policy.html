Privacy Policy for SkillTrack

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SkillTrack ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account (email address), log practice sessions (duration, notes, skill names), and create habits.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) from Google.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including to personalize your experience, track your progress, and communicate with you.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Google OAuth for optional sign-in.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@skilltrack.app.
